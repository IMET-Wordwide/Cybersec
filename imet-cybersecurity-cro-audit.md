
# IMET Worldwide CRO Audit & 4-Step Plan — Online DBA in Cybersecurity

October 22, 2026 · CRO Audit

## Read This First

The IMET Worldwide Online DBA in Cybersecurity page has several opportunities to improve conversion by clarifying program information, removing repeated content, strengthening trust, and making the enquiry journey easier to follow.

The priority is not changing button colours. It is ensuring that a prospective doctoral candidate can quickly understand the qualification, tuition, eligibility requirements, academic arrangements, and next steps before submitting an enquiry.

The most important findings are:

1. **Conflicting scholarship information.** The page advertises scholarships of up to 45%, while an FAQ states up to 40%. This discrepancy should be resolved before testing scholarship messaging.
2. **Qualification and awarding-body clarity.** The page promotes a doctorate and the “Dr.” prefix, but its institutional description requires clearer information about the degree-awarding institution and the exact qualification awarded.
3. **Repeated eligibility content.** Educational Background and Professional Experience information is repeated, making the page longer without necessarily helping applicants make decisions.
4. **Unsubstantiated outcome claims.** Salary growth potential, consulting-rate increases, and executive career outcomes need supporting evidence or clearer qualifications.
5. **Inconsistent terminology and errors.** The hero contains “Scholarships & Feed,” and several sections contain awkward wording, inconsistent capitalization, or unclear technical terminology.
6. **Multiple conversion paths.** Advisor enquiries, brochure downloads, scholarship checks, and enrolment CTAs compete for attention without a clearly communicated primary action.
7. **Repeated program content.** Benefits, industry information, research support, and other sections contain overlapping material that could be consolidated.
8. **Trust and recognition questions.** Applicants need transparent, verifiable information about academic recognition, accreditation claims, eligibility, and degree conferral.

No traffic, conversion, CRM, or A/B testing data was supplied. Therefore, this audit identifies observed content issues and proposed experiments; it does not claim a measured conversion loss or guarantee a conversion lift.

## A/B Experiments — Step 4 Only

Run experiments after the factual and technical fixes. Test one major variable at a time where practical, and measure qualified enquiries rather than clicks alone.

| # | Test | Control → Variant | Primary Metric | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X1 | Hero messaging | Existing introduction → concise audience and applied-research-focused introduction | Qualified enquiry rate | Bounce rate and lead quality |
| X2 | Decision information | Current information layout → clear tuition, scholarship conditions, duration, and eligibility summary near CTA | Form completion rate | Qualified-lead share |
| X3 | Testimonial placement | Testimonials in current positions → three verified, relevant testimonials near the enquiry form | Qualified enquiry rate | Page performance and lead quality |
| X4 | Form friction | Existing verified form → shorter form with only necessary initial fields | Form completion rate | Qualification rate and spam submissions |
| X5 | CTA consistency | Current CTA wording → consistent primary advisor CTA | Successful form submissions per visitor | Brochure downloads and qualified leads |
| X6 | Content consolidation | Existing repeated sections → shorter, consolidated content with the same essential information | Qualified enquiry rate | Engagement with key program details |

### Experiment Rules

- Define the control, variant, audience, primary metric, and guardrail before launching each test.
- Use the current conversion rate and available traffic to estimate the required sample size.
- Choose the minimum detectable effect based on business value rather than assuming a particular lift.
- Avoid ending tests early because one version temporarily appears to perform better.
- If traffic is insufficient for a reliable experiment, ship verified usability or factual corrections and monitor results without claiming statistical proof.
- Do not test contradictory tuition or recognition claims. Correct them before experimentation.
- Preserve lead quality and privacy compliance while reducing friction.

## Measurement Framework

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Advisor CTA click rate | Unique advisor CTA clicks divided by eligible page visitors |
| Form start rate | Visitors who begin the enquiry form divided by eligible page visitors |
| Form completion rate | Successful form submissions divided by form starts |
| Visitor-to-enquiry rate | Successful enquiry submissions divided by eligible page visitors |
| Qualified-lead rate | Leads meeting the agreed qualification criteria divided by total leads |
| Brochure download rate | Successful brochure downloads divided by eligible page visitors |
| Advisor attendance rate | Completed advisor calls divided by scheduled calls |
| Mobile conversion rate | Successful enquiry submissions divided by eligible mobile visitors |

Define each metric consistently in the analytics platform before reporting results. Compare equivalent time periods and account for changes in traffic sources, campaigns, and seasonality.

## Not Checked — Requires Additional Evidence

- Actual page speed and Core Web Vitals.
- Current form fields, abandonment, validation errors, and consent defaults.
- Whether every CTA and anchor works correctly.
